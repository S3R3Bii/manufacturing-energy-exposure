# Sources and established method

- [Eurostat PEFA metadata](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/EN/env_pefa_sims.htm) and [env_ac_pefa04](https://doi.org/10.2908/env_ac_pefa04): NACE Rev.2 physical energy accounts, including net domestic energy use in terajoules. Preserve the source boundary and flags; this is not an energy-bill dataset.
- [Annual national accounts metadata](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/cache/metadata/en/nama10_esms.htm) and [nama_10_a64](https://doi.org/10.2908/nama_10_a64): gross output P1 at current and previous-year prices, million euro, and official chain-linked indices. Previous-year-price figures permit matched-price annual comparisons but are not a homogeneous level series. Chain-linked component levels generally cannot be added.
- [Official Statistics API query contract](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/user-guides/data-browser/api-data-access/api-detailed-guidelines/api-statistics): JSON-stat 2.0 dimension order, repeated dimension filters and paired sinceTimePeriod/untilTimePeriod filters. Retain exact source bytes and never refresh during a reproduction run.
- [Eurostat reuse notice](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/main/help/copyright-notice): statistical data and metadata may be reused for commercial/non-commercial purposes with source attribution, subject to stated exceptions. Verified 29 October 2026.
- B. W. Ang (2005), The LMDI approach to decomposition analysis: a practical guide, Energy Policy 33:867-871, DOI 10.1016/j.enpol.2003.10.010. LMDI is established; no new-method claim is made. The publisher abstract was available in discovery; direct full-text retrieval was unavailable.
- Economidou, M. and Roman-Collado, R. (2019), [Assessing the progress towards the EU energy efficiency targets using index decomposition analysis in 2005-2016](https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/bitstream/JRC115210/kjna29665enn.pdf), JRC115210, DOI 10.2760/61167. This primary policy-research report documents activity, structure and intensity decomposition. It supplies method context; this study uses its own fixed panel and does not reproduce the report's EU policy estimates.

No source paper is redistributed. Retained source files will contain only eligible official aggregate statistical API responses and their selected metadata. Historical snapshots are current revisions, not a reconstruction of what analysts knew during 2015-2023.
