# Data attribution and reuse

Source: Eurostat, physical energy flow accounts (env_ac_pefa04) and annual national accounts by industry (nama_10_a64), accessed 29 October 2026. The exact retrieval timestamps and update labels are retained with each response.

Eurostat's [reuse notice](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/main/help/copyright-notice) authorizes reuse of published statistical data and metadata with source acknowledgement, subject to its stated exceptions. The European Commission implements its reuse policy through Decision 2011/833/EU. This package uses official aggregate data and selected factual metadata; it does not redistribute third-party source papers, photographs, identifiable private records or restricted microdata.

Modifications: the original API responses are preserved byte-for-byte in compressed form. Derived tables select the declared countries, years, industries and measures, retain flags/exclusions, and calculate original accounting contrasts and figures. These calculations are independent research; Eurostat has not endorsed them.
